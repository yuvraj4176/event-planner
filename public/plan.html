    Steps 2 and 3: Your plan

    When you create your plan it appears below the form, ready to review and track.

    • Budget breakdown that adds up exactly, with a tracker for what you spend.
    • Dated timeline that adapts when your event is closer than ideal.
    • Vendor checklist with booking deadlines and questions to ask.
    • Personalised ideas based on your theme, guests and budget.
    • Ask the planner for quick answers about your plan.

    Your plan stays in this browser. Nothing is saved on the server.

    Clear saved plan?

    This removes your plan, checked tasks and spending from this browser. It cannot be undone.
